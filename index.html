Help Center

Find answers to common questions and get the support you need

How do I place an order?

Browse our products, add items to your cart, then click "Proceed to Checkout." You'll need to create an account or sign in, fill in your shipping details, and confirm your payment. You'll receive an order confirmation once complete.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All transactions are secured with SSL encryption.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available for an additional fee. Free shipping is offered on orders over $100. International shipping times vary by destination.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. Items must be in their original condition with tags attached. Simply contact our support team or initiate a return from your account dashboard. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also check the status in your account under "My Orders." Our tracking system provides real-time updates on your delivery.

Can I change or cancel my order?

You can modify or cancel your order within 1 hour of placing it. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed. Contact our support team immediately if you need to make changes.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by location. Import duties and taxes may apply depending on your country's regulations.

How do I contact customer support?

You can reach us via WhatsApp at +20 101 959 3092, call us at +20 110 102 5358, or email support@luxe-store.com. Our team is available 24/7 to assist you with any questions or concerns.
